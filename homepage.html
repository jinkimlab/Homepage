Academic & Industry Profile

Prof. Jin-Chul Kim has built a long-standing academic and industrial career spanning pharmaceutical science, drug delivery systems, colloid and interface science, and polymer science. He has served as professor at Kangwon National University since 2003 after industrial research experience at LG Household & Health Care and postdoctoral research at Purdue University.

His laboratory develops advanced carriers designed to improve therapeutic efficacy while minimizing side effects. Major interests include liposomes, cubic phases, polymeric carriers, nanostructured biomaterials, functional cosmetic formulations, and stimuli-responsive delivery systems triggered by light, temperature, pH, electric current, magnetic fields, and specific biochemical signals.

The lab aims to connect strong fundamental science with translational value in pharmaceuticals, cosmeceuticals, and biomedical materials.
